# Why was my credit card rejected during {{site.data.keyword.cloud_notm}} upgrade?
{: #ts_credit-card}
{: troubleshoot}

Your credit card was rejected when you upgraded to a {{site.data.keyword.cloud_notm}} Pay-As-You-Go account.
{: shortdesc}

When you go **Manage** > **Account** > **Account Settings** in the {{site.data.keyword.cloud_notm}} console, the following message is displayed:
{: tsSymptoms}

```text
Could not place order. Problem authorizing the credit card. We are unable to process your request: Transaction Rejected.
```

Something went wrong while your account upgrade was being processed and the upgrade couldn't be completed. 
{: tsCauses}

For information that is specific to your upgrade request, contact the {{site.data.keyword.cloud_notm}} Trust Enablement team at verify@us.ibm.com.
{: tsResolve}
